A home-grown tokenizer wins at home

Sarvam-1 averages on the ten Indian languages it was built for. Outside that list it costs much more: on Urdu and on Chinese. Its English also takes more tokens than GPT-4o's. A low tax only means a language costs little compared with English on the same tokenizer. It does not make the tokenizer cheap.

Method

Token counts come from each tokenizer's own files: tiktoken, or a Hugging Face tokenizer.json. Special tokens are not counted. Tax = total tokens in a language ÷ total tokens in English, over 1,012 parallel sentences, with a 1000-draw bootstrap interval.

Limits

FLORES is formal text. Romanized Hindi is machine-transliterated. Fewer tokens does not mean a better model, and prices change, so we report tokens, not rupees.
